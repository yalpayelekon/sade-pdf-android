# Play Console setup

The app metadata, bilingual listing, privacy policy, icon, feature graphics and phone screenshots are prepared. The signed upload bundle is built locally at `app/build/outputs/bundle/release/sade-pdf-0.1.2.aab`.

The Play Console's create-app form is filled with the app name, package ID, app type and free price. It requires the account owner to personally attest to Developer Program Policy compliance and US export-law compliance before it creates the app. Those declarations are intentionally left unchecked.

After the owner completes those attestations:

1. Create the app with package ID `io.github.yalpayelekon.sadepdf`.
2. Upload the signed `sade-pdf-0.1.2.aab` bundle.
3. Add the English listing from `LISTING.md`; add Turkish as a translated listing.
4. Upload `assets/play-icon-512.png`, both feature graphics, and the two phone screenshots.
5. Add the privacy policy URL from `LISTING.md` and complete App content, Data safety, content rating, ads, app access and target audience forms truthfully.
6. Select the launch countries and regions.

Do not submit a production release. If the Play Console requires a closed test for this personal developer account, that is the separate 12-tester / 14-day phase and is intentionally outside this preparation.
